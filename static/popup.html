X Feed Lens

Click Feed context under any text post on your X For you timeline to see how many of the other posts you actually saw are about the same subject, in the post's own language and across all languages, and whether that changed since the previous week. A multilingual language model bundled with the extension does the comparison on this device; post text never leaves it.

A post counts once per tab session when at least half of it stays on screen for one second. Stored on this device for 30 days: post ID, author handle, X's language tag, matched catalog topics, the post's visible text (up to 4,000 characters, for local analysis), a short excerpt, and the meaning vectors computed from that text.
